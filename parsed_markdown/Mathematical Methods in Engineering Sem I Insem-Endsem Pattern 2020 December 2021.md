# Q.1

A particular type of printer ribbon is produced by only two companies, Alamo Ribbon Company and South Jersey Products. Suppose Alamo produces 65% of the ribbons and that South Jersey produces 35%. Eight percent of the ribbons produced by Alamo are defective and 19% of South Jersey ribbons are defective. A customer purchases a new ribbon. What is the probability that South Jersey produced the ribbon? What is the probability that Alamo produced the ribbon?

- Truck C is a modified form of truck B. It has 20-ton payload, but requires a crew of one man, which reduces its payload capacity to 17 tons, while raising the cost to Rs. 120,000.

Truck A could run for an average of 20 hours a day. Trucks B and C require a crew of two men each and if driven on three shifts per day, could run for 19 hours and 22 hours respectively.
