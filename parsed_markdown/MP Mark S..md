# 3.9) Working with Thermoforming

# a) Schematic Explanation of the Steps

A sheet of plastic material is heated to pliability, then pressed against a 3D mold by vacuuming out the air between the sheet and the mold.

The sheet and the mold are then subjected to air pressure above the sheet.

# b) Applications

| Mostly suitable for       | Complex shapes & tighter tolerances. |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Chain - Injection Molding | Cardboard / Plumbing fittings        |
| Extrusion                 | Cosmetic packaging - 11- C8x0.5-4MAS |
| Food & beverage bottles   | Blow molding                         |
| Automobile fuel tanks     | Vacuum thermoforming                 |
| Luggage bags              | Vacuum thermoforming                 |
| Refrigerator inner panels |                                      |
