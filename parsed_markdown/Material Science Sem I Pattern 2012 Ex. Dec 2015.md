# 1. Explain the use of Ni base and Cobalt base alloy for high temperature application.

Nickel base and cobalt base alloys are primarily used in high temperature applications due to their excellent mechanical properties and resistance to oxidation and corrosion at elevated temperatures. These alloys maintain their strength and stability in extreme environments, making them suitable for components in gas turbines, jet engines, and other high-performance applications.

# 2. Explain the properties of superconductors.

Superconductors are materials that exhibit zero electrical resistance and the expulsion of magnetic fields when cooled below a certain critical temperature. Key properties include:

- Zero electrical resistance
- Meissner effect (expulsion of magnetic fields)
- Critical temperature, above which superconductivity is lost

# 3. Briefly explain two applications of superconductors.

Two notable applications of superconductors are:

- Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) machines, which utilize superconducting magnets to produce high-quality images.
- Particle accelerators, where superconducting materials are used to create powerful magnetic fields necessary for accelerating particles to high speeds.

# 4. Discuss the effect of Cryogenic heat treatment on steel alloy.

Cryogenic heat treatment involves cooling steel alloys to cryogenic temperatures, typically below -196°C. This process can enhance the material's properties by transforming retained austenite into martensite, improving hardness, wear resistance, and dimensional stability. Additionally, it can reduce residual stresses and improve fatigue resistance.
